Direct booking flow

Home

1 "Check availability" stays in the bar on a phone while the four links fold into the menu, so the one action is always one tap away.

2 The headline carries the reason to book here in its own sentence. A guest comparing tabs reads this line and little else.

3 The date bar sits above the rooms because every later price depends on it. With dates set, each price is a total and no longer a "from".

4 Rooms show a "from" price for guests who browse before choosing dates. Tapping a row opens that room.

5 Location and Questions are sections here, not pages: the date bar stays one scroll away. Does the hotel have a restaurant or tours that need a section?

Rooms for your dates

1 Inside the booking flow the step indicator replaces the global links. Earlier steps are links back; later ones are disabled.

2 The total for the stay with taxes leads each card, with "Select" beside it. A guest can book from this list without opening a room.

3 A taken room stays in the list with its next free dates and a way to see them. Hiding it would leave the guest with no path.

Room

1 The breakdown shows every line that makes up the total, then how it splits into the deposit and the balance, so nothing new appears at payment.

2 Cancellation terms sit beside the price, before any commitment, with the actual date.

3 The total and the action stay fixed to the bottom of the screen while the guest scrolls the room.

Your details

1 The stay is repeated at the top with "Change", so a wrong date is fixed here and not discovered after paying.

2 Three required fields and two optional ones. No account, no address, no marketing checkbox. The email field is drawn in its error state.

3 The button names the next step, and the line under it states what is paid now and what is paid at the hotel.

Booking confirmed

1 Reference and email destination come first: the two things a guest checks before closing the tab.
